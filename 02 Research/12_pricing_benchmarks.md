# 12 — B2B SaaS Pricing Benchmarks (Indian Sports/Education Sector)

> **Purpose:** Market pricing reference for whenever Rit's deliberately-deferred pricing decision resumes. This is a benchmark to inform future decision-making, not a basis for choosing a price now — pricing remains deferred per existing decision (see `RIT_PLAN.md`, Business Model section).

---

## 1. School Management SaaS — Per-Student Pricing

| Product | Model | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Generic School ERP | Per-student/month | ₹5–50/student/month |
| Inkrant | Base + per-student tiers | Mini ₹999/mo (≤100 students); Pro ₹5,999 + ₹39/student |
| Schoolites | Registration + per-student | ₹3,000 one-time + ₹10/student/month |
| Edacify | Per-student/month (minimum cohort) | ₹50–70/student/month, min. 100–150 students |
| School Ease | Per-student/month | ₹4–8/student/month |
| MySmartSchool | Per-student/month | ₹5/student/month (annual billing) |

**Observed range: ₹4–50/student/month**, with lower-tier products clustering around ₹4–10.

## 3. Sports-Specific / PE-Specific SaaS

Limited public pricing data exists here — most relevant products (SmartPE India, KhiladiPro, SportsMechanics) do **not publicly list pricing**. This is itself a useful finding: **direct competitors in Rit's specific space have not established public pricing norms**, meaning there's no strong anchor pressure toward a particular number yet.

---

## 4. Pricing Model Comparison

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-child | Scales with enrollment, predictable for schools | May discourage large enrollments at high per-child rates | Schools with variable enrollment |
| Per-coach | Stable, aligns with coach-centric workflows | Doesn't scale with student growth | Academies with stable coach counts |
| Per-institution (flat) | Simple, predictable | Can be expensive for small institutions | Larger, established institutions |
| Freemium | Low barrier to entry | Risk of non-paying user base | User-acquisition phase |
| Government contract | Large scale potential | Slow procurement, compliance burden | Only after track record established |

---

## 5. Direct Implications for Rit (When Pricing Resumes)

- **Per-coach pricing** fits Rit's actual product shape better than per-student, given the coach-first B2B model already decided — most benchmarked competitors are per-student (school-ERP-style), which doesn't map cleanly onto Rit's coach-centric workflow
- **Tier-2/2.5 affordability ceiling** appears to sit toward the lower end of observed ranges (₹4–10/student/month equivalent, or the ArenaFlow-style ₹289/month entry tier) — useful anchor for a future low-end pricing test, not a decision
- The **absence of public pricing** among Rit's closest direct competitors (SmartPE, KhiladiPro, SportsMechanics) means Rit has real flexibility — no strong market anchor is pulling toward a specific number
- **Freemium framing already fits** the existing "free coach training as adoption driver" decision in `RIT_PLAN.md`'s Business Model section (Option D from the original pricing-shape discussion)

**This remains benchmark information only.** Per the existing decision, exact pricing stays deferred until product validation.
