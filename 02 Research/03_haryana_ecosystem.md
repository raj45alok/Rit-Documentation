# 03 — Haryana Ecosystem: Infrastructure, State Model & Candidate Institutions

> **Purpose:** Evidence base for the decision to launch Rit's pilot in the Haryana belt (Bhiwani, Sonepat, Panipat, Karnal, Hisar), and a concrete institution shortlist for outreach.

---

## 1. Why Haryana — State-Wise Performance Comparison

### Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) medal table
| State | 2025 rank | 2025 medals | 2024 rank | 2024 medals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maharashtra | 1 | 158 | 1 | 158 |
| Haryana | 2 | 117 | 3 | 103 |
| Rajasthan | 3 | 60 | Outside top 3 | — |
| Karnataka | 4 | 58 | Outside top 3 | — |
| Tamil Nadu | 5 | 75 | 2 | 98 |
| Kerala | 8 | 25 | 9 | 35 |
| Manipur | 9 | 30 | 10 | 31 |
| Punjab | 13 | 44 | 8 | 39 |

**Caution:** rank is driven by gold medals, not total medals; a low rank does not prove low participation — it may reflect population size, selection depth, or which sports were contested that year.

### What Haryana does differently — the transferable model
- **2,000 sports nurseries** established to identify and train talent early
- **Monthly athlete scholarships:** ₹1,500 (ages 8–14), ₹2,000 (ages 15–19)
- **25 residential sports academies** with a reported ₹500/day diet allowance
- Long-standing cash rewards, government jobs, and pensions for high-achieving athletes
- **Policy logic:** broad local feeder base (nurseries) → reduced dropout pressure (scholarships/diet support) → progression infrastructure (residential academies) → credible incentive (rewards/jobs)

This is the closest real-world proof-of-concept for the "continuity" thesis underlying Rit's product (see `RIT_PLAN.md`, Problem Statement 5).

## 2. SAI & Government Infrastructure in the Target Districts

| Institution | District | Sports | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAI Ch. Devi Lal Northern Regional Centre | Sonepat | Archery, Athletics, Hockey, Kabaddi, Wrestling | Regional centre, multi-discipline |
| SAI National Boxing Academy (NBA) | Rohtak (near Bhiwani) | Boxing (residential) | ~60 athletes at inauguration; ages 10–25 via talent hunts |
| SAI Training Centre (STC) Hisar | Hisar | Athletics, Badminton, Boxing, Hockey, Judo, Wrestling | Part of 69 STCs nationally (4,556 athletes total) |
| SAI Training Centre (STC) Bhiwani | Bhiwani | Multi-discipline | Part of 69 STCs nationally |
| Motilal Nehru School of Sports (MNSS), Rai | Sonepat | Athletics, Archery, **Badminton, Boxing, Swimming**, + 10 more disciplines | Boarding school, 100 seats at Class 5 intake |

**MNSS Rai covers all four of Rit's launch sports under one roof** — the standout candidate for a single-institution pilot partnership rather than stitching together four separate relationships.

### Khelo India Centres (KICs) in the belt (illustrative, not exhaustive)
Haryana has **22 KICs across 18 districts** (20 operational). Notable in-belt examples: Charkhi Dadri boxing club (35 trainees), Jind athletics centre (37 trainees), Sonepat multi-discipline centre (boxing/judo/kabaddi/wrestling/wushu).

## 4. Recommended Pilot Targeting

| Priority | Target | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | MNSS Rai, Sonepat | All 4 sports under one institution; government-affiliated; established, contactable |
| **Secondary/parallel** | Bhiwani Boxing Club | Elite-reputation private coach (Dronacharya awardee); boxing-specific credibility and trust |
| **Anchor city consideration** | Sonepat over Bhiwani | Sonepat has the single densest public infrastructure footprint in the whole belt (SAI Regional Centre + NCOE + MNSS + multiple KICs) |

**Contact starting points:** MNSS Rai — mnssrai.com; Principal's office 0130-2366501; Admin Officer 0130-2366201. SAI centre contacts via SAI's official directory (Deputy Director listings per STC).

## 5. Known Trade-off

Tier-2/2.5 geography (chosen over Tier-1 metros to avoid competing with existing hybrid-academy players like Game Theory/Machaxi) means a **thinner existing pool of already-credentialed coaches** compared to a metro. Coach acquisition and the coach-training module carry more early weight here — Rit may need to help *create* qualified coaches, not just onboard existing ones.
