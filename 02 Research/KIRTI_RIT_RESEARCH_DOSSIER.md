# KIRTI × RIT Research Dossier

## Khelo India Rising Talent Identification --- System, Technology, Gaps, and Integration Opportunities

**Research status:** Working dossier\
**Last updated:** 9 September 2026\
**Purpose:** Understand KIRTI end-to-end and identify where Rit can
complement it before, during, and after talent identification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Executive Summary

KIRTI (Khelo India Rising Talent Identification) is a Government of
India initiative under the Khelo India ecosystem focused on identifying
and nurturing sporting talent among children aged 9--18.

KIRTI was launched in March 2024. Current government material reports
174 Talent Assessment Centres (TACs) and 1,87,921 assessments, with
KIRTI using standardized assessment protocols and advanced IT tools
including AI and data analytics.

### Initial strategic conclusion

**Rit should not try to become another KIRTI.**

KIRTI's core strength is **talent identification at scale**.

Rit's broader mission can potentially sit around the larger journey:

> **Discover → Start Playing → Participate Consistently → Get Coached →
> Get Assessed → Develop → Reassess → Compete → Progress**

A useful working model is:

``` text
                    KIRTI
             Talent Identification
                      │
          ┌───────────┴───────────┐
          │                       │
       BEFORE                  AFTER
          │                       │
       RIT MAY                 RIT MAY
       HELP CHILD              HELP CHILD
       REACH KIRTI              DEVELOP
          │                       │
          ▼                       ▼
   Discovery / Access       Coaching / Training
   Participation            Progress Tracking
   Parent Support           Facilities
   School Support            Competition
   Readiness                Reassessment
```

This is a **hypothesis**, not yet a validated product decision.

# 2. What KIRTI Is

KIRTI is intended to identify sporting potential among young people and
create a pipeline into structured athlete development.

The official KIRTI concept describes a **pyramidal structure**,
beginning at grassroots identification and progressing toward
development of elite athletes.

It also proposes creating **"Bharat Benchmarks"** from assessment data
to provide standardized performance reference points.

Source: SAI KIRTI Concept Document\
https://sportsauthorityofindia.gov.in/sai/public/assets/pdfs/DPAC/45th%20DPAC/UPDATED%20DPAC%20ANNEXURES.pdf

### Target age

Official sources describe KIRTI as targeting children approximately
**9--18 years old**.

### Launch

KIRTI was launched in **March 2024**. The second phase was launched in
July 2024.

Source: PIB\
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2034329

# 4. KIRTI Assessment Model

The publicly documented assessment system combines:

1.  General physical/fitness tests
2.  Sport-specific tests
3.  Standardized protocols
4.  Centralized data
5.  Talent identification/selection processes

Publicly documented general tests include:

-   Height
-   Weight
-   Sit and Reach
-   Standing Vertical Jump
-   Standing Broad Jump
-   Medicine Ball Throw
-   30m Sprint
-   4×10m Shuttle Run
-   Sit-Ups Endurance
-   Age-dependent endurance testing such as 800m/1600m

Sport-specific testing is also used.

Publicly documented KIRTI sports include disciplines such as:

-   Archery
-   Athletics
-   Badminton
-   Boxing
-   Football
-   Hockey
-   Kabaddi
-   Kho-Kho
-   Volleyball
-   Weightlifting
-   Wrestling

**Do not assume this is the exhaustive current list.** Future research
must distinguish currently active disciplines from historical/planned
coverage.

# 5. Bharat Benchmark

The original KIRTI concept proposes creating **"Bharat Benchmarks"**
from large-scale standardized assessment data.

Conceptually:

``` text
Many assessments
      ↓
Standardized measurements
      ↓
Large national dataset
      ↓
Reference distributions
      ↓
Bharat Benchmarks
      ↓
Better talent-development decisions
```

Rit should **not** recreate a competing national benchmark system.

If permitted, Rit could potentially consume official assessment outputs
as one input into a broader development profile.

functionality. They do **not automatically prove that every feature is

# 8. The Most Important Question: What Happens After Assessment?

This should become one of Rit's highest-priority research questions.

The ideal development loop is:

``` text
ASSESS
  ↓
UNDERSTAND
  ↓
RECOMMEND
  ↓
TRAIN
  ↓
TRACK
  ↓
REASSESS
  ↓
ADJUST
  ↓
PROGRESS
```

Public evidence is much stronger around **assessment and
identification** than around a complete, child-level, longitudinal
digital development loop.

That makes the post-assessment journey a potentially important area for
investigation.

# 9. BEFORE KIRTI --- Potential Rit Layer

## 9.1 Sports discovery

A child may not know:

-   which sport fits their interests
-   where they can play
-   which facilities are nearby
-   which coaches are trustworthy
-   how much participation costs
-   how to start as a beginner

Potential Rit flow:

``` text
Age + interests + location + availability
                 ↓
          Sport discovery
                 ↓
      Local opportunity discovery
                 ↓
        First participation
```

This must be validated against existing Khelo India/private ecosystems.

## 9.2 Participation before talent identification

KIRTI identifies potential. Rit could potentially help create the
participation habit:

``` text
No participation
      ↓
Try a sport
      ↓
Play regularly
      ↓
Get coached
      ↓
Build basic skills
      ↓
KIRTI assessment
```

This is closely aligned with Rit's original mission.

## 9.3 Parent education

Potential parent-facing support:

-   why sport matters
-   realistic expectations
-   injury prevention
-   age-appropriate training
-   nutrition basics
-   avoiding premature specialization
-   understanding assessment results
-   understanding that non-selection is not failure

# 10. DURING KIRTI --- Potential Complementary Layer

Rit should not interfere with official assessment or selection.

Potential support:

### Before assessment

-   appointment reminders
-   venue information
-   preparation guidance
-   consent support
-   parent education
-   assessment history

### During assessment

Potentially:

-   participant profile
-   consent records
-   communication
-   personal assessment history

Any official assessment-data integration would require authorization.

### After assessment

A potentially more interesting experience is:

> "Here are your strengths, areas to develop, nearby opportunities, and
> a 12-week participation/development pathway."

rather than simply:

> "Selected / Not selected."

Whether KIRTI already provides such a service must be verified.

# 14. Non-Selected Child Pathway

This may become one of Rit's strongest hypotheses.

Conceptual current problem:

``` text
Assessment
   ↓
Selected? ─── YES → Development
   │
   NO
   ↓
What next?
```

Potential Rit pathway:

``` text
Assessment
   ↓
Selected? ─── YES ──→ Structured athlete pathway
   │
   NO
   ↓
Development recommendations
   ↓
Continue participation
   ↓
Local coaching
   ↓
Progress tracking
   ↓
Reassessment
```

**This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed KIRTI gap.**

It must be validated through interviews and current KIRTI documentation.

# 17. What Rit Should NOT Build

Rit should avoid:

1.  A duplicate KIRTI assessment platform.
2.  An unofficial selection authority.
3.  "AI predicts the next Olympic champion."
4.  A duplicate national athlete registry.
5.  A generic AI fitness chatbot.
6.  A sports social feed with no meaningful outcome.

# 20. Child Safety and Privacy

requirements:

-   parental/guardian consent
-   child safeguarding
-   role-based access control
-   minimal data collection
-   encryption
-   audit logs
-   data retention rules
-   deletion/correction processes
-   safe coach-child communication
-   safeguarding escalation
-   secure media storage

# 21. Pilot Options

## Pilot A --- Before KIRTI

**Goal:** Help children discover and consistently participate in sport
before assessment.

``` text
School/community
→ Sport discovery
→ Local opportunity
→ Participation
→ Coach
→ KIRTI readiness
```

## Pilot B --- After KIRTI

**Goal:** Help assessed children continue developing.

``` text
KIRTI assessment
→ Rit profile
→ Development recommendation
→ Coach/facility
→ Training
→ Progress
→ Reassessment
```

## Pilot C --- Coach ecosystem

**Goal:** Connect young athletes with verified grassroots coaches.

``` text
Child
→ Sport
→ Verified coach
→ Facility
→ Training
→ Progress
```

**Current hypothesis:** Pilot B may have the strongest strategic fit,
but this should be decided only after validation.

# 22. Hypotheses to Validate

1.  Children who are assessed but not selected still need a structured
    next step.
2.  Parents need help interpreting assessment results.
3.  Coaches need actionable information from assessment data.
4.  Families struggle to find trustworthy youth coaches.
5.  Children drop out between identification and sustained training.
6.  Longitudinal tracking is insufficient.
7.  Schools/community organizations need simpler sports-opportunity
    discovery.
8.  Coaches need better development tools than raw test scores.
9.  Children need easier access to local facilities after assessment.
10. Reassessment is not connected well enough to everyday training.

# 25. Current Strategic Thesis

A potentially strong thesis for Rit is:

> **KIRTI identifies potential. Rit could potentially help convert
> potential into sustained participation and development.**

More broadly:

> **KIRTI is one node in a larger youth-sports journey; Rit should
> investigate whether it can build the continuity layer around
> discovery, participation, coaching, development and reassessment.**

This is deliberately **not a final product decision**.

# 27. Final Product Principle

Rit should not ask:

> **"How do we add AI to KIRTI?"**

The better question is:

> **"Where does the youth-sports journey break around KIRTI, and can Rit
> solve one of those problems substantially better?"**

That keeps Rit aligned with:

**Problem → Evidence → Validation → Product → Technology → Scale**

rather than:

**Technology → Feature → Product → Search for a problem.**
