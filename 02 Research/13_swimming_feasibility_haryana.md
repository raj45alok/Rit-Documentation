# 13 — Swimming Feasibility: Haryana District-by-District (Correcting an Earlier Assumption)

> **Purpose:** This document exists specifically to correct a load-bearing assumption in `RIT_PLAN.md` — that pond/lake access is an acceptable substitute for pool access at the grassroots level. **That assumption is not supported by evidence and has been reversed.**

---

## 1. Headline Finding

**Natural water bodies (ponds/lakes/canals) are not suitable for organized youth swimming training, talent identification, or timed assessment** due to safety, hygiene, and standardization concerns. This directly overturns the prior plan decision. Swimming in the Haryana MVP must be **partner/facility-dependent**, not a universal fourth sport delivered the same way as Athletics, Badminton, and Boxing.

---

---

## 3. Why Open Water Was Ruled Out

- Drowning risk — natural water bodies carry significant, well-documented risk with no standard safety infrastructure
- No lifeguard infrastructure, no standardized safety protocols
- No consistent depth, current, or hygiene conditions — cannot support reliable, comparable assessment
- Legal/safety restrictions apply to swimming in natural water bodies in Haryana generally

**This does not mean swimming is impossible in the MVP — it means the delivery model must change.**

---

## 4. Revised Conclusions

**A. Is swimming viable in the Haryana MVP?** Partially — highly partner-dependent, not universally deliverable across all five target districts.

**B. If yes, where?** Sonepat (SAI Northern Regional Centre) is the only currently verified viable location.

**C. Should swimming be removed, delayed, or made partner-dependent?** **Made partner-dependent.** Swimming should launch specifically through the Sonepat/SAI relationship, not as a district-agnostic fourth sport delivered identically everywhere Athletics/Badminton/Boxing are delivered.

**D. Minimum partner/facility requirements for any swimming site:**
- Verified pool with safety equipment
- Certified lifeguards
- Qualified swimming coaches
- Parental consent and supervision protocols
- Emergency response plan

**E. Non-negotiable safety requirements:** lifeguard presence, coach certification, parental consent, safety equipment, emergency protocols. **None of these can be satisfied by an open-water/pond-lake site.**
