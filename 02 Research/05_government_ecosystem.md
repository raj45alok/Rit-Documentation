# 05 — Government Ecosystem: Policy, Programs & the Regulatory Landscape

> **Purpose:** Map of the government policy and program landscape Rit must position itself relative to — NEP 2020, Khelo India, KIRTI, and the proposed NCAB. This is the primary reference for "build complementary, not competing" decisions throughout the plan.

---

## 1. National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

- First Indian education policy to treat sport as **integral to experiential learning**, not extracurricular
- Proposes **40 minutes of daily sport** built into the school day, in partnership with local academies, community volunteers, and coaches
- This is essentially the "grassroots delivery mechanism" Rit is trying to strengthen — but under-executed in practice
- A 2026 academic paper argues Indian PE remains marginalized and unevenly implemented despite the policy signal — the **policy-practice gap is a documented research theme**, not just Rit's own hypothesis

---

## 2. National Sports Policy / Khelo Bharat Niti 2025

- Launched August 2025; focus on inclusive development — tribal communities, SC/ST groups, women, economically weaker sections
- Aims to bridge rural-urban access gap for training and facilities
- **326 new grassroots infrastructure projects approved** (~₹3,124 crore)
- **1,045 district-level Khelo India Centres established** — but density is still thin against 700+ districts and 1.5 million schools

---

## 3. Khelo India Mission & Talent ID and Development (KITD)

| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| Children assessed via Khelo India Mobile App | 23+ lakh (5–18 age range) |
| PE teachers trained to assess sporting prowess | 82,000+ |
| Accredited academies | ~266 |
| Khelo India Athletes (KIA) support | Up to ₹5 lakh/year + monthly Out of Pocket Allowance |

**Important distinction:** these are program-output figures, not independent evaluations of coaching quality, talent-ID validity, or satisfaction.

---

## 4. KIRTI — Khelo India Rising Talent Identification

**The most important recent finding for Rit's positioning — a major government program not initially accounted for in early competitor scanning.**

- Launched March 2024
- **AI-powered**, national scale
- **Ages 9–18**
- **11 disciplines**: Athletics, Badminton, Boxing, Archery, Football, Hockey, Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, Volleyball, Weightlifting, Wrestling
- Target: **20 lakh (2 million) assessments/year**
- Operates via **Talent Assessment Centres** — periodic drives, ~120 athletes/day, district-as-unit-of-assessment
- Backed by ₹3,442 crore sports budget
- Integrated with NSTS, FIT India Portal, NSRS, eKhelpathshala
- **Strong early traction in Haryana specifically** — 2nd-highest state by assessment volume in Phase 1 (after Maharashtra)
- **National goal:** top-10 sporting nation by 2036, top-5 by 2047

### What KIRTI does NOT do (Rit's opening)
- Does not cover ages 6–9
- Operates as a periodic scouting *event*, not continuous coaching/development
- Does not train the coaches/PE teachers who staff its own assessment centres (a stated operational need)
- **Swimming is not among its 11 disciplines** — genuinely open ground

See `RIT_PLAN.md`, "Relationship to KIRTI" section for the full positioning decision (Rit as pre- and continuous layer around KIRTI, not a competing system).

---

## 5. National Coach Accreditation Board (NCAB) — Proposed

See `02_coach_credentialing.md` for full detail. Summary: task force reported Dec 2025 (led by Pullela Gopichand), proposes National Coach Registry + Accreditation Portal + LTAD-based standards, piloting with 3–4 NSFs as of mid-2026, not yet nationally live.

---

## 6. Health & Nutrition Policy Infrastructure

- **School Health & Wellness Programme** (Ayushman Bharat, launched Feb 2020) — two trained Health and Wellness Ambassadors per government school, 1 hour/week interactive health promotion across 11 themes including nutrition
- **Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram (RKSK)** — adolescent health program including nutrition, anaemia, counselling, parent engagement
- **Documented gap:** existence of curriculum ≠ universal classroom delivery or adequate teacher training

---

## 7. Governance & Execution Context

- **NITI Aayog (2024) governance review**: sports development responsibilities split across 10+ agencies with limited coordination
- **Fund utilization gap**: under 40% of the National Sports Development Fund's 2022–23 allocation was actually utilized for athlete development — indicating execution capacity, not just budget size, is a bottleneck
- **No comprehensive statutory framework** governing sports administration — policy currently relies on executive discretion rather than binding legal mandate (per 2026 institutional-analysis literature)

---

## 8. Summary: Rit's Positioning Relative to Government Infrastructure

| Government system | What it does | Rit's relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Khelo India App | Free fitness assessment | Complementary — Rit's Tier 1/2 sits on top of, not duplicating |
| KIRTI | Periodic AI talent-scouting, ages 9–18 | Complementary — Rit covers 6–9 gap + continuity between drives + trains KIRTI's own assessment staff |
| NCAB (proposed) | National coach accreditation | Complementary — Rit builds NCAB-compatible infrastructure, not a rival authority |
| NEP 2020 | Policy mandate for daily school sport | Rit is one possible *delivery mechanism* for an under-executed policy intent |

**Core principle across all four:** Rit does not seek to replace or compete with any government system — it builds the development layer between participation, assessment, coaching, and progression.
